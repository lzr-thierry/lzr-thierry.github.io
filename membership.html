Membership

The Singapore Section of the Combustion Institute (SSCI) welcomes all researchers, engineers, students, and professionals with an interest in the field of combustion, whether you are based in Singapore or overseas. By becoming a member, you join a vibrant community dedicated to advancing combustion science and technology in Singapore and beyond.

Why Join the SSCI?

SSCI Members Are Also CI Members

All SSCI members also enjoy the member benefits of The Combustion Institute (CI), including eligibility for all CI awards and fellowships and substantial discounts on symposia and publications.

See all CI member benefits

Meeting Discounts

Reduced registration fees at one to two SSCI meetings a year, plus CI member rates at CI-organized conferences like the International Symposium on Combustion (ISOC).

Awards and Prizes

Eligibility for the Young Investigator Award, with up to two awardees a year chosen by an international panel.

Part of the CI Network

Members are on the same member database as The Combustion Institute (CI) and receive CI news.

Industry Connection

Workshops bringing together companies, investors and AI researchers.

Online Lectures and Workshops

Lectures and workshops on specific topics, such as AI for science and ammonia and hydrogen combustion, given by invited experts and open to members.

A Voice in the SSCI

Vote at general meetings and stand for election to the Committee.

How to Join

  1. Apply and Pay Online

    Fill in the online membership form and pay your subscription in the same form: by bank transfer, or by PayNow if you are in Singapore. The form shows the payment details. Students and retired members, please include proof of status.

  2. Receive Your Certificate

    The Committee checks your application and payment, then sends you your membership certificate.
