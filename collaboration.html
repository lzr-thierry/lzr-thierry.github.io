Young Researcher Exchange Programme

Connecting the Next Generation of Combustion Researchers

The SSCI Young Researcher Exchange Programme aims to strengthen connections between young combustion researchers in Singapore and neighbouring Sections of The Combustion Institute.

Through this programme, the Singapore Section of The Combustion Institute (SSCI) works with partner Sections to create opportunities for graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career researchers to participate in each other's annual meetings and scientific activities.

Our Objectives

The programme aims to:

  • Promote scientific exchange among young combustion researchers in the region;
  • Provide opportunities for young researchers to present their work to an international audience;
  • Encourage networking, research collaboration, and exchange of ideas;
  • Strengthen connections between SSCI and neighbouring Sections of The Combustion Institute; and
  • Build a sustainable regional network for the next generation of combustion scientists and engineers.

How the Exchange Works

SSCI collaborates with participating Sections of The Combustion Institute to invite young researchers to attend and present their work at the SSCI Annual Combustion Meeting and other selected scientific activities.

Where available, SSCI may provide selected visiting young researchers with financial support and/or registration fee waivers to facilitate their participation.

In return, young researchers from Singapore may be nominated to participate in annual meetings and activities organized by our partner Sections.

Partner Sections

The programme is being developed in collaboration with neighbouring Sections of The Combustion Institute across the Asia-Pacific region.

Current and future exchanges may include:

  • Korean combustion community / KOSCO

Additional partner Sections and exchange opportunities will be announced as the programme develops.

Building Long-Term Connections

The Young Researcher Exchange Programme is more than a conference visit. SSCI hopes that these exchanges will help young researchers establish lasting professional relationships, initiate new research collaborations, and become actively involved in the international combustion community.

By connecting emerging researchers across national and institutional boundaries, SSCI seeks to contribute to a stronger and more closely connected Asia-Pacific combustion research community.

Interested in Participating?

Young researchers interested in future exchange opportunities are encouraged to follow SSCI announcements or contact us for further information.

We also warmly welcome other Sections of The Combustion Institute interested in establishing a young researcher exchange with SSCI to get in touch with us.
