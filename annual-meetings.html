SSCI Annual Meetings

SSCI Annual Combustion Meetings bring together researchers and industry partners to share advances in combustion science and technology.

2nd SSCI Annual Combustion Meeting

Date: November 16, 2026

Following the highly successful inaugural meeting, the second annual meeting will serve as a forum for researchers, practitioners, and students from academia, industry, and government to present recent advances in combustion research, exchange ideas, and establish new collaborations. More details are found on the official conference website .

1st SSCI Annual Combustion Meeting

Date: December 9, 2025

This annual Meeting was a key event for combustion researchers, engineers, and students in Singapore. It featured technical sessions, a poster session, and opportunities for collaboration. More details are found on the official conference website .
