Meeting Venue & Transportation Guide

Second SSCI Annual Combustion Meeting

Date: 16 November 2026
Address: Level 2 Theatrette, CREATE Tower, 1 Create Way, Singapore, 138602

Interactive Map

By Public Transportation

Via MRT

The most convenient MRT station is Kent Ridge MRT Station (CC24) on the Circle Line.

  • Exit via Exit A and follow the sheltered walkway towards CREATE Tower. The walk takes approximately 5–8 minutes.

Via Bus

Several public bus services stop near CREATE Tower and the National University of Singapore (NUS), including 95, 96, 151, 183, and 188. From the nearest bus stop, CREATE Tower is only a short walk away.

By Car or Taxi

Driving Directions

CREATE Tower is located at 1 Create Way, Singapore 138602, within the University Town / CREATE campus area. Drivers may access the area via the Ayer Rajah Expressway (AYE) and follow signs towards CREATE Tower.

Taxi / Private Hire Drop-off

For taxi or ride-hailing services, simply set the destination to "CREATE Tower" or "1 Create Way, Singapore 138602". The main entrance provides a convenient drop-off point.

Visitor Parking

Visitor parking is available at CREATE Tower and nearby public car parks. Parking charges apply according to the prevailing rates.

  • Recommended: Use the visitor parking facilities at CREATE Tower.
  • Alternative: Additional parking is available within the nearby NUS University Town area.

Please follow on-site signage and parking instructions upon arrival.
